# AI TrialRoom

**Try it on before you buy it.** A digital trial room for saree shops, menswear showrooms and shoppers.

Developed by **Gundal Business Pvt Ltd**, Bengaluru · gundalg@gmail.com

## Download the app
Open **Releases** on this page, then download the latest `AI-TrialRoom-v1.0.x.apk` to your Android phone.
Open it, and allow "Install unknown apps" when the phone asks.

## What it does
- Shop owner or individual sign-in, mobile number verification, and a photo-use agreement
- Shop branding: logo, name and address on every photo, with 3 themes (Royal Maroon, Peacock Emerald, Rose Ivory)
- Customer photo or AI model (3 skin tones, female and male)
- Female: saree, lehenga, salwar kameez, kurti, party gown, office formal
- Male: two- and three-piece suit, tuxedo, formal shirt and trousers, blazer and chinos, sherwani, kurta pyjama
- Official dress codes: business formal, business casual, office daily, boardroom, black tie, ethnic formal
- Upload a saree or fabric photo to use its pattern, or pick colours from 1-inch swatches for every part
- Showroom backgrounds: silk saree gallery, bridal lounge, tailor studio, corporate lobby, studio light
- On-device AI body detection: photos never leave the phone, and the face is kept exactly as in the original photo

## Quality
Every change runs `tests/regression.py` (81 checks across all outfits, sarees, shirts, suits, AI models, themes and error cases) before the APK is built.
